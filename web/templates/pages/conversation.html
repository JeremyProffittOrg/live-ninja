            Help

            Live Ninja is a hands-free voice assistant. You talk, it answers out loud, and it remembers what matters between conversations.

            Getting started

            • Press the mic button (🎙) in the left rail and start talking. Press it again to stop.
            • Or go hands-free: turn on Always listening next to the mic button and say your wake phrase — the phrase in use is shown right under the switch.
            • Prefer to type? Use the box at the bottom of the transcript. Typed and spoken turns land in the same conversation.
            • The pill at the top right of the rail tells you where you are: Idle, Listening, Thinking, or Speaking.
            • Just start talking again to interrupt a reply — you don't have to wait for it to finish.
            • Start over with NEW, at the left of the orb row. It ends the current conversation and begins a fresh one.
            • The bar along the bottom is always there, on any screen size: Show Conversation, then History, Memory and Downloads, and an Audio picker.
            • On a phone or tablet the voice panel fills the screen. Tap Show Conversation to open the conversation full screen — the same button then reads Hide Conversation. You can also swipe up from the voice panel and scroll back down to return to it.

            What you can ask for

            Live Ninja does more than answer questions — it can act on your behalf.

            • Weather and time for your saved places, so you never have to spell out where you are.
            • Look things up on the web — a quick answer, or a longer piece of research.
            • Remember and forget things — people, places, projects, tasks and plans. Ask it to remember something and it sticks across devices.
            • Timers and reminders — "set a timer for ten minutes", "remind me on Friday".
            • Send an email with what you just worked out.
            • Make files for you — notes, documents and bundles that show up under Downloads.
            • Control the device it's running on where that's supported, including volume, camera and photos.
            • Start a fresh conversation or stop listening, just by saying so.

            Turn on Show tool calls under the persona picker in the rail to watch these actions happen in the transcript.

            Where everything lives

            History
            Every conversation, tagged by topic. Filter by topic, device, or date, and see what each conversation cost.
            Memory
            Everything Live Ninja remembers about your world. Edit any of it, or make it forget. Guides are standing instructions applied in every conversation, on every device.
            Personas
            How Live Ninja talks. Pick a built-in personality, write your own, or share yours. Each persona carries its own voice — switch personas from the picker in the rail. Alongside the character personas there are three working ones — Product Owner, Staff Engineer and Staff SRE — for thinking through software with you. They will tell you when they think you're wrong, say why, and offer the alternative.
            Downloads
            Files Live Ninja has created for you. Files are kept for 180 days; share links expire after about 15 minutes.
            This month
            Your running usage estimate, at the bottom of the Settings panel. The badge beside the state pill shows what the current conversation has cost so far.
            Show Conversation
            The button at the bottom left opens the conversation full screen. The same button then reads Hide Conversation; the ✕ and Esc close it too.
            The bar along the bottom
            Always on screen, at every window size: Show Conversation, then History, Memory and Downloads, and an Audio picker. It stays put while the conversation is open, so you can close it from the same place you opened it, and it is the only place these live — the rail no longer carries a second copy. Audio sets how quickly Live Ninja treats a pause as the end of your turn (the same setting as Mic pickup under Turn detection), and Mic test… in that list opens the microphone check.
            Copy, Screenshot and Tag for review
            Above the conversation, and inside the overlay. Copy puts the whole conversation on your clipboard as plain text; Screenshot saves it as a PNG image; Tag for review asks why it's worth another look and keeps that note on this device.
            Help and Settings
            Two small tabs stacked in the top-left corner: ⚙ for Settings, and ? for this panel directly beneath it. Hover either one to see what it is.

            Settings explained

            Open the ⚙ tab in the top-left corner. Changes save automatically for the device you're on — open Device settings in any section to compare devices or copy that section elsewhere.

            About you
            What Live Ninja knows before you say anything — your name, your home and work places, and facts worth keeping. Picking a place from the list is what makes weather and local time reliable. Suggestions it makes while you talk wait here for your approval.
            Wake word
            The phrase that starts a hands-free conversation. Choose from the built-in phrases, and where training is available, add your own (2–4 words works best).
            Persona
            How Live Ninja speaks and behaves. Pick a preset or write your own instructions.
            Voice engine
            Which speech-to-speech model powers your conversations. All engines share the same tools, memory, and transcripts — only cost, latency, and voice quality differ.
            Turn detection
            How it decides you've finished speaking. Semantic VAD judges from meaning and is the recommended setting; Server VAD simply waits for a fixed silence gap. Mic pickup sensitivity and how long it keeps listening after a reply live here too. The Audio picker in the bottom bar changes mic pickup without opening Settings, and the Android app has the same Low/Med/High control above its conversation.
            Appearance
            Light or dark theme, the visual style of the app and of the live panel, and your accent colour.
            Microphone
            Which input this browser uses. Device names only appear after you've allowed microphone access once.
            Privacy
            Whether transcripts are stored and for how long — 7, 30, or 90 days, or not at all.
            Account
            Name your devices so settings and history are easy to tell apart, or sign out of this browser or everywhere.

            Keyboard and navigation

            • Esc closes this panel, the Settings panel, and any dialog.
            • Clicking the dimmed area outside a panel closes it too.
            • Tab moves through controls; focus stays inside a panel while it's open and returns to the tab that opened it when you close it.
            • Enter sends what you've typed in the message box.

            Tips and troubleshooting

            • It can't hear you? Choose Mic test… from the Audio picker in the bottom bar — it shows your live level and the exact steps to unblock the microphone in your browser.
            • Wake phrase not triggering? Raise the wake sensitivity in Settings → Wake word, or pick a longer phrase. Very short phrases false-trigger and very quiet rooms need more sensitivity.
            • It cuts you off mid-sentence? Switch turn detection to Semantic VAD, or lower the mic pickup sensitivity.
            • Changed persona or voice mid-conversation? That applies to the next conversation — start a new one to hear it.
            • Settings look different on another device? Most settings are per device on purpose. Use Device settings in a section to apply it everywhere.
            • Want it to stop remembering something? Ask it to forget, or delete the entry on the Memory page.
            • Something went wrong in a conversation? Use Tag for review and say what happened, then Copy or Screenshot it — the note stays on this device, so send the copy or the image along if you're reporting it.
            • Copy did nothing? Some browsers only allow the clipboard on a secure page you've interacted with — tap the button again, and if it still refuses, use Screenshot instead.

            Troubleshooting tips
            • Flat line / no level? Check the mic's physical mute switch and that it's plugged in, then speak at normal volume about an arm's length away.
            • Chrome blocked the mic: click the tune (or lock) icon at the left of the address bar → Site settings → set Microphone to Allow, then reload this page. You can also open chrome://settings/content/microphone and remove live.jeremy.ninja from the "Not allowed" list.
            • Wrong microphone? Chrome records from your system default mic unless you pick one in the ⚙ Settings panel (Microphone section). On Windows, change the default under Settings → System → Sound → Input.
            • Windows is blocking desktop apps: Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone — turn on microphone access and "Let desktop apps access your microphone".
            • Another app is holding the mic (shows as "could not open the microphone"): close Zoom, Teams, OBS, or other recording apps and test again.
            • Level moves here but the assistant doesn't respond: raise the input volume in Windows Sound settings, and make sure you speak after the status pill says Listening.
